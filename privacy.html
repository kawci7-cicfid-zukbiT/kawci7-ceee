2. The data actually processed by this Service

The Service has no user accounts and no login. It does not ask for your name, address or any registration. The data involved is the following.

2.1 Advertising — Google AdSense

The Service displays ads through Google AdSense. When you consent (see section 6), Google and its advertising partners may set and read cookies and similar identifiers to serve and measure ads — including personalised ads — processing data such as your IP address, browser and device information, and your interactions with ads. The AdSense script is loaded from pagead2.googlesyndication.com. How Google uses this data is described in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services”, and you can control your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com.

2.2 Content you choose to submit — Google Firebase (Firestore)

If you add, save or share materials, packaging structures or laminates, that information is stored in a cloud database (Google Firebase / Firestore, project wvtr-community). What is stored depends on what you enter, and may include: material name, family, supplier/company name, technical barrier values (WVTR/OTR), test conditions, a contact email you type into a material entry, and the laminate structures you save. Material entries you share with the community become visible to other users of the Service. Laminates you save are stored under an anonymous identifier (see 2.4) and are not published.

Please do not enter personal data or confidential information you are not entitled to share (for example, do not put a third party’s personal email in a shared material entry).

2.3 Abuse protection — Google reCAPTCHA & Firebase App Check

To stop the community database from being abused, the Service uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 together with Firebase App Check. These collect device and usage signals for security and fraud prevention. They are provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms.

2.4 Data stored on your own device (local storage)

To work, the Service keeps some data in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not advertising data:

KeyPurpose
wvtr_uidAn anonymous identifier (e.g. anon_…) generated at random to link the laminates you save to the cloud, without identifying you.
wvtr_user_votesRemembers the reliability votes you cast on materials.
wvtr_monthly_statsLocal usage counters used to show “most used” materials.
wvtr_mat_trashMaterials you deleted, kept locally so you can restore them.

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

2.5 Technical libraries loaded from CDNs

The Service loads technical libraries from content delivery networks — Chart.js (charts), jsPDF and html2canvas (PDF/report export), and the Firebase SDK. Loading these is technically necessary to display the page and may expose your IP address to the relevant CDN as part of the standard request.
