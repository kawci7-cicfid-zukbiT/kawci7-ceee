Created by Lorenzo Venturelli

Lorenzo Venturelli is an Italian industrial chemist with expertise in packaging materials, barrier films and laminate structures. He created BarrierLab out of a practical need: packaging engineers spend too much time running physical tests on structures that could be screened digitally in minutes.

The goal was simple — build a rigorous, free, integrated tool that covers the complete design workflow, from calculating the combined WVTR of a multilayer film all the way to checking ICH regulatory compliance and sizing the desiccant inside a pharmaceutical container. No installation. No paywall. No data sent to any server.

BarrierLab is more complete than single-purpose calculators like the Norner WVTR tool because it connects all the steps engineers actually need: barrier calculation → temperature extrapolation → shelf life prediction → compliance check → desiccant sizing — in a single, coherent workflow.

What BarrierLab can do

🧮

WVTR & OTR Laminate Calculator

Build a multilayer film layer by layer and compute the combined Water Vapor Transmission Rate and Oxygen Transmission Rate using the series resistance model. Supports hygroscopic correction for EVOH and Nylon, and fixed-barrier treatment for metallised films (MET-PET, AlOx, SiOx).

📈

Sensitivity Analysis

Sweep one layer's thickness across a range while locking all others. Reveals where material changes have real impact, where you hit a performance plateau, and where thinning a layer creates critical risk — the engineering "knee" in the barrier curve.

🌡️

Arrhenius Temperature Extrapolation

Enter WVTR or OTR measured at two or more temperatures. Extracts activation energy Eₐ (kJ/mol), reports R² goodness-of-fit, and predicts barrier performance at any target storage temperature — without running a third test.

🌍

ICH Q1A(R2) Compliance — MVTR Engine

Evaluates pharmaceutical packaging compliance across all 7 ICH climatic zones (Zone I through IVb, Accelerated, Intermediate). Gives pass/fail per zone, safety margin percentage, years-to-limit, and sensitivity analysis on key inputs.

⏱️

Shelf Life Predictor

Predicts product shelf life from barrier WVTR or OTR combined with product degradation parameters. Moisture mode uses dynamic GAB isotherm simulation with non-linear declining driving force. Oxygen mode uses a zero-order lipid oxidation model.

💨

Headspace O₂ Evolution

Simulates how oxygen concentration evolves inside a sealed package over time, balancing film OTR ingress against product respiration. Supports zero-order and first-order kinetics, Arrhenius and Q10 thermal models, and logistics chain scenarios.

🧊

Desiccant Sizing Calculator

Calculates the desiccant mass required inside a pharmaceutical container from three moisture sources: film permeation, headspace air at sealing, and product desorption. Covers silica gel, molecular sieves (3Å / 4Å), montmorillonite clay and calcium chloride with full sorption isotherm data.

🌿

Carbon Footprint Calculator

Estimates cradle-to-gate CO₂ equivalent emissions of a laminate structure in g CO₂eq per m² and per finished package unit. GWP data from PlasticsEurope Eco-profiles and Ecoinvent 3.x, aligned with ISO 14040/14044 and ISO 14067.
