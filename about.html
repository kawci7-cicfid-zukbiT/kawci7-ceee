What is BarrierLab?

BarrierLab is a free, browser-based platform created by Lorenzo Venturelli, industrial chemist, to help packaging engineers and scientists design and validate multilayer barrier films without running a physical test at every design iteration.

It covers the full engineering workflow in a single integrated tool: from calculating the WVTR or OTR of a laminate structure, to predicting whether it will keep a pharmaceutical product stable across global climatic zones, to sizing the desiccant needed inside the container.

Tools

WVTR & OTR Laminate Calculator

Build a multilayer film layer by layer. Computes combined Water Vapor Transmission Rate and Oxygen Transmission Rate using the series resistance model (Fick's Law). Supports hygroscopic correction for EVOH and Nylon, and fixed-barrier treatment for metallised films (MET-PET, AlOx, SiOx).

Sensitivity Analysis

Sweeps one layer's thickness across a range while locking all others. Reveals where material thickness changes have the most impact, where you hit a plateau (bottleneck is elsewhere), and where reducing thickness creates critical risk — the engineering "knee" in the barrier curve.

Arrhenius Temperature Extrapolation

Enter WVTR or OTR measured at two or more temperatures. The tool fits the Arrhenius equation, extracts activation energy Eₐ (kJ/mol) and the pre-exponential factor A, reports R² goodness-of-fit, and predicts performance at any target temperature.

ICH Q1A(R2) Compliance — MVTR Engine

Evaluates pharmaceutical packaging compliance across all 7 ICH climatic zones: Zone I (21°C/45%RH) through Zone IVb (30°C/75%RH), plus Accelerated and Intermediate. Gives pass/fail, safety margin (%), years-to-saturation, and sensitivity analysis on Eₐ and WVTR input.

Shelf Life Predictor

Predicts product shelf life from the barrier WVTR or OTR and product degradation parameters. Moisture mode: dynamic day-by-day GAB isotherm simulation with non-linear declining driving force. Oxygen mode: zero-order lipid oxidation model. Both support Q10 and Arrhenius thermal acceleration.

Headspace O₂ Evolution

Simulates how oxygen concentration evolves inside a sealed package over time, balancing film OTR ingress against product respiration. Supports zero-order and first-order kinetics, Arrhenius and Q10 thermal models, and a logistics chain mode for multi-step supply chain scenarios.

Desiccant Sizing Calculator

Calculates the desiccant mass required inside a pharmaceutical container. Accounts for film permeation ingress, headspace moisture at sealing, and product moisture desorption. Covers silica gel (Type A and B), molecular sieves (3Å and 4Å), montmorillonite clay, and calcium chloride, each with full sorption isotherm data.

Carbon Footprint Calculator

Estimates cradle-to-gate greenhouse gas emissions of a laminate structure. Reports results in g CO₂eq per m² (for comparing structures) and g CO₂eq per finished package unit (for sustainability reporting). GWP data from PlasticsEurope Eco-profiles and Ecoinvent 3.x.
