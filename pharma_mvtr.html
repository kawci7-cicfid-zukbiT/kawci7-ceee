Mechanics of MVTR Analysis & ICH Q1A(R2) Compliance

The Moisture Vapor Transmission Rate (MVTR, also written WVTR) is the steady-state flux of water vapor through a unit area of packaging film under defined conditions of temperature and relative humidity.

I. The ICH Climatic Zone Framework

The ICH codified the global climatic landscape into five zones, each representing the mean kinetic temperature and relative humidity conditions a pharmaceutical product encounters during its commercial life.

Zone Definitions — ICH Q1A(R2) / WHO TRS No. 863:
Zone I (21°C / 45% RH) — Temperate · Zone II (25°C / 60% RH) — Subtropical
Zone IIIa (40°C / 15% RH) — Hot/Dry · Zone IVa (40°C / 75% RH) — Hot/Humid
Zone IVb (30°C / 75% RH) — Hot/Very Humid (ASEAN)
Accelerated (40°C / 75% RH) · Intermediate (30°C / 65% RH)

II. Arrhenius Temperature Correction

F_T = exp [ (Eₐ / R) × (1/T_ref − 1/T_target) ]
R = 8.314 × 10⁻³ kJ/(mol·K) · T in Kelvin

III. Relative Humidity Driving Force

F_RH = RH_target / RH_ref
WVTR_eff = WVTR_ref × F_T × F_RH

IV. Cumulative Ingress

Ingress_daily (mg) = WVTR_eff × A (m²) × 1000
Ingress_total = Ingress_daily × t_shelf (days)
Compliance: Ingress_total ≤ M_crit
